Replit Build Prompt — Front-End Audit Form Field Fix
Repo: baldjc/attraction-by-video (the public-facing landing page — NOT the member site)
File: artifacts/abv-landing/src/components/AuditForm.tsx
Scope: One-line bug fix. ~2 minutes of work.
Risk: Very low — purely a key-name correction.

The Problem
The audit form posts to GHL with two commission fields. They're currently named inconsistently:
tsCurrent_YouTube_Commission: formData.currentCommission,   // capital C
desired_YouTube_Commission: formData.desiredCommission,   // lowercase d — WRONG
This breaks the lead flow two ways:

In GHL — the inconsistent casing creates confusion between desired_YouTube_Commission and Desired_YouTube_Commission custom fields. Depending on how the GHL workflow is wired, the "desired commission volume" value is landing in the wrong field or not mapping at all.
In the member-site webhook — the backend endpoint at /api/webhooks/ghl/audit-request/route.ts specifically reads body.Desired_YouTube_Commission (capital D). So when the GHL Custom Webhook action forwards the payload to the member site, the lowercase-d version is ignored and the field comes through as null.

Net effect: the desired commission field silently disappears from every lead.

The Fix
Open artifacts/abv-landing/src/components/AuditForm.tsx and change line 59 from:
tsdesired_YouTube_Commission: formData.desiredCommission,
To:
tsDesired_YouTube_Commission: formData.desiredCommission,
(Capital D, matching the pattern of Current_YouTube_Commission on the line above.)
That's the whole change. No other files need to be touched.

Verify
After the fix, search the file to confirm there's only one esired_YouTube_Commission match and it starts with a capital D:
bashgrep -n "esired_YouTube_Commission" artifacts/abv-landing/src/components/AuditForm.tsx
Expected output (one line):
59:          Desired_YouTube_Commission: formData.desiredCommission,
